    HOW TO PLAY

    🖱️
    Steer. Your serpent always follows the pointer (or your finger). It never stops moving — plan turns early; big serpents turn slower.
    ⚡
    Boost. Hold click / Space / the ⚡ button for ~1.8× speed. It burns your length and drops orbs behind you — a trade, not a freebie. You can't boost when tiny.
    🔮
    Eat. Orbs are pulled magnetically toward your mouth. The big bright orbs are the remains of dead serpents — worth far more than ambient dust.
    ⚔️
    Fight. Nobody can touch your body and live. Boost across a rival's path so their head hits your side. Head-on collision? The longer serpent wins — ram small ones, dodge big ones.
    🎁
    Power-ups. Grab the glowing rings: ⚡ free boost for 6s · 🧲 huge orb pull for 10s · 🛡️ survive one death · 💠 instant +20 length. Bots want them too.
    🧬
    Evolve. At length 40 / 110 / 210 / 340 you change form — bigger, faster, and visibly scarier. A crowned serpent with fins earned it.
    🧱
    The wall. The red ring is electrified and kills instantly (only a 🛡️ shield saves you). Never let a fight push you into it.
    🏆
    Win. Camp crash sites for orb feasts, encircle smaller serpents to trap them, and check the minimap so a Leviathan never surprises you.

    ABOUT THIS GAME

    Neon Serpent Arena is an original game inspired by snake.io and slither.io. The genre DNA — grow by eating, boost burns mass, head-vs-body kills — comes from those classics. Everything else was rebuilt from scratch in vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

    What's different here
    • Honest AI — named bots with readable logic, not fake "multiplayer" pretending to be humans.
    • Circular arena with a lethal electrified wall — no safe edges to hide along.
    • Deterministic duels — head-on crashes are won by size, not reaction-time luck.
    • Evolution — 5 forms that change your size, speed and look as you grow.
    • Contested power-ups — overdrive, magnet, shield and feast, hunted by bots too.
    • Magnetic orbs, particle death-bursts, and a parallax neon world.
    • 100% static & offline — no servers, no ads, no tracking, no downloads. One tiny page.
